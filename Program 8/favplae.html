My Favourite Place

SWITZERLAND

Switzerlandofficially called the Swiss Confederation, is a small country in Central Europe made up of 16,000 square miles of glacier-carved Alps, lakes and valleys.

The water is just bluer in Switzerland, said Claudio Zemp of Switzerland Tourism, the marketing organization for the national and international promotion of the country as a vacation, travel and convention destination. The air is just crisper – again, it sounds super corny, but you can feel it, really. You see the meadows are green. Everything is so neat. It's a little bit of a paradise in Switzerland.
